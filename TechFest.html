Where Code, Culture and Competition Collide

About the Fest

LPU CodeCarnival is the flagship annual technical and cultural fest organised by the students of the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Lovely Professional University. Spread across three action packed days, the fest brings together coders, designers, gamers and performers from every hostel block and academic department for a celebration of technology and talent.

From 24-hour hackathons to robotics duels, from open-mic nights to a closing DJ concert, CodeCarnival is built to give every student — technical or not — a reason to step out, participate, and be seen. Entry is free for all registered LPU students on production of a valid University ID card.

Who can attend CodeCarnival?

All currently enrolled LPU students, from any school or hostel block, can attend and participate by carrying their official University ID card at the entry gate.

Is there a participation fee for technical events?

Most events are free to attend. A small nominal fee applies only to a few flagship competitions such as the hackathon and robotics duel, covering kits and refreshments.

Key Highlights & Features

  • 24-Hour Hackathon: Build a working prototype overnight and pitch it to industry judges.
  • Robotics Duel: Student-built bots battle it out in the arena.
  • Competitive Programming Contest: ICPC-style problem solving with live leaderboard.
  • Design & UI Sprint: Rapid prototyping challenge for aspiring designers.
  • Gaming Zone: BGMI, FIFA and Valorant LAN tournaments.
  • Cultural Night: Dance, music and fashion show finale.
  • Mega Food Court: Stalls featuring regional and street-style cuisines.
  • Celebrity DJ Concert: A star-studded live closing performance.

Why You Should Not Miss It

CodeCarnival is more than a fest — it is a resume-building opportunity. Winners of the hackathon and coding contest receive certificates, cash prizes, and direct interview referrals from our recruiting partners. Even if you are here just to enjoy the food, music and games, you will leave with new friends from hostel blocks you have never visited before.

Volunteers get a certificate of appreciation, and every participant receives a digital participation badge that can be added to your LinkedIn profile.
